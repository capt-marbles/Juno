# GTM Side Panel Concept

A GTM side panel is a foreground review/control surface for background ambient GTM work.

## Core sections

### Daily GTM Inbox

A queue of items needing attention:

- account trigger detected
- competitor update found
- CRM hygiene issue
- launch comment needs reply
- content opportunity
- outbound draft awaiting approval

Each item should include source, confidence, why it matters, suggested action, and approval buttons.

### Account Briefs

For selected accounts:

- company summary
- recent signals
- contacts
- known pain points
- tech stack hints
- prior outreach
- relevant product angles
- suggested next touch

### Draft Review Queue

A safe place for human approval before external action:

- emails
- LinkedIn messages
- Slack/community replies
- CRM updates
- docs/content edits
- campaign copy

Actions: preview, edit, approve, reject, regenerate with feedback.

### Competitor Watch

- launches
- pricing/package changes
- docs changes
- hiring signals
- customer complaints
- positioning shifts
- suggested talk tracks

### Launch War Room

- HN/Reddit/X/GitHub mentions
- sentiment clusters
- unanswered questions
- bug/doc gaps
- suggested responses
- hourly summary
- post-launch retrospective
